# Khomba

### Chomi, sihambe.

**Everyday taxi travel, more connected.**

Khomba is a South African mobility product being built to help commuters connect with minibus taxi drivers and make everyday journeys simpler, more convenient and easier to follow.

[Explore Khomba](https://khomba.co.za) · [Meet the founder](https://github.com/Boitybanks) · [Start a conversation](https://github.com/Boitybanks/Khomba/discussions)

## Why Khomba exists

Getting home should not mean standing in the rain, uncertain about your next taxi. For people travelling to work, school or home, the daily commute deserves better visibility and connection.

Khomba brings a digital connection to the minibus taxi journey, building around a transport system that already serves our communities.

## The product experience

| For commuters | For minibus taxi drivers |
| --- | --- |
| Find a taxi travelling towards your destination. | Make your availability visible to commuters. |
| Request a ride through the app. | Review requests and coordinate pickups. |
| Follow the taxi’s approach and your journey. | Connect with passengers who need your service. |

## Built for South African journeys

From the early morning commute to the journey home, Khomba’s vision is to make affordable everyday transport easier to access for underserved communities, while supporting the drivers who keep them moving.
